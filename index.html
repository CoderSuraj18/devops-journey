Learning Journey

Day 1 — Linux Fundamentals

WSL and Ubuntu, pwd, ls, mkdir, touch, PATH, shell built-ins, and external commands.

Day 2 — Linux Filesystem

Linux filesystem hierarchy, absolute and relative paths, cd, cat, files, and directories.

Day 3 — Linux Permissions

chmod, chown, chgrp, users, groups, permissions such as 755, 644, 700, 600, and umask.

Day 4 — Processes & Monitoring

ps, jobs, bg, fg, Ctrl+Z, kill, kill -9, top, htop, free, df, and du.
